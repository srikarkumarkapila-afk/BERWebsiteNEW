EV 4

EV 4 — Generation IV

Our fourth-generation electric contender featured dual EMRAX motors, torque vectoring capability, a 588V tractive battery pack, and a planetary drivetrain system.

System Specification
Motor EMRAX 188 HV LC (Dual Inboard)
Peak Power 60 kW @ 6500 RPM
Peak Torque 100 Nm
Motor Controller Cascadia Motion PM100DZ
Accumulator Cells Molicel P30B (INR)
Pack Capacity 7.056 kWh (588V Full Charge)
Differential Torque Vectoring (Planetary 3.72)
FSAE Michigan: #16 Overall Endurance: Finished Design: Top 10
EV 3

EV 3 — Generation III

Our third-generation electric race car featured key technical advancements in motor technology, custom lithium-ion battery integration, and limited-slip drivetrain mechanics.

System Specification
Motor Emrax 208 MV (Axial Flux)
Peak Power 86 kW @ 6000 RPM
Peak Torque 150 Nm
Motor Controller Cascadia Motion PM100DX
Accumulator Cells Molicel P45B (INR)
Pack Capacity 5.44 kWh (357V Full Charge)
Differential Drexler V3 Limited-Slip
Acceleration: 3.8s Autocross: Cleared
