What is Formula SAE?

Formula SAE is a student design competition organized by SAE International. The competition challenges university teams from around the world to conceive, design, fabricate, and compete with small, formula-style electric and combustion racing cars. Teams are evaluated not just on vehicle speed and endurance on the track, but also on engineering design, manufacturing cost, and a comprehensive business presentation.
